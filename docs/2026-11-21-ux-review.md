# UX review: desktop UI (M1 preview screenshots)

Scope: the 13 PNGs in `.superpowers/wt-ui/docs/screenshots/`. I read the egui source in
`crates/ui-egui/src/` only where a still image was ambiguous. Floating tool windows and
"preview" sample data are expected in headless renders, so they are not counted as issues.
The renders are 2x, so pixel sizes below are halved to logical points.

Totals: **4 High, 13 Medium, 7 Low** (24 findings).

Severity: **High** means a likely mis-click or misread that puts the wrong audio, or silence,
on air, or makes the operator miss a fixed event. **Medium** slows the operator or confuses
them under time pressure. **Low** is polish or consistency.
Effort: **S** < half a day, **M** 1–3 days, **L** more than that.

---

## Ranked findings

### 1. Skip and Fade on the on-air deck: one click, no guard, same look as on idle decks — High · S–M
- **Window:** main window, deck strip (`ui-preview.png`, `decks.rs` 145–180).
- **What I see:** Deck A (ON AIR) has ⏸ ⤵ ⏭ ⚙ as identical 28 pt icon buttons, about 4 pt
  apart. Decks B, C and D show the same row with ▶ instead of ⏸. Skip and Fade send their
  command on the first click. There's no arm or hold step and no confirm. Only the tooltip
  says what they do. ⚙ sits right next to ⏭.
- **Why it hurts:** a stray click near ⚙ (to change the fade time), or a mis-aimed click
  meant for Pause, cuts the song on air. The ⤵ glyph renders like a plain down arrow, so
  Fade reads as "load" or "move down". On a ready deck these buttons are harmless. On the
  live deck they are destructive, yet they look the same on both.
- **Fix:**
  - On a live deck, give Skip and Fade a guard: either press-and-hold (about 400 ms) or arm
    then click again within 2 s. Show the armed state with a fill.
  - Draw them with the `warn` / `danger` edge only on the live deck.
  - Move ⚙ away from the transport group, to the far left of the header or into the badge
    menu, and leave a gap of at least `space::M`.
  - Put text under the glyphs ("Fade", "Skip") or use a clearer fade glyph.

### 2. No countdown to the next fixed event in the main window; remaining time is the smallest text on screen — High · S
- **Window:** main window, deck strip and clock (`ui-preview.png`).
- **What I see:**
  - The top right shows wall-clock time `14:58:00` (26 pt) and `AUTO`.
  - The time left on the on-air item is `0:51 / 2:51 · −2:00`, in 12.5 pt muted mono, the
    smallest and dimmest text on the card.
  - "News at 15:00 is in 2:00" appears only in the separate Clock window.
  - In the Main list, the fixed News divider is a thin 20 pt row in small blue text.
- **Why it hurts:** "how long until the hard event" and "how long left on this song" are the
  two numbers an operator checks most often. With the Clock window closed, or on another
  screen, they have to subtract in their head.
- **Fix:**
  - Under the station clock, add a line such as `⏱ News in 2:00 (hard)` using `theme::LEAD`
    or larger. Turn it `warn` below 60 s and `on_air` below 10 s, with the text changing
    too, not only the colour.
  - On the live deck, make the remaining time the largest number on the card (for example
    `−2:00` at `SECTION` size), with elapsed/total secondary.

### 3. Delete / Backspace removes rows with no confirm and no undo, and the on-air row isn't excluded — High · M
- **Window:** main pane (`context-menu.png`, `main_pane.rs` 165–206, `context_menu.rs` 77–93).
- **What I see:** With the Main pane focused, `Delete` or `Backspace` removes every selected
  row at once (`Edit::Remove(sel)`). Text fields do swallow the keys. Even so:
  - The UI doesn't ask for confirmation.
  - The main pane has no undo.
  - The UI doesn't stop the on-air row or locked rows from being removed.

  The context menu's "Remove" has the same effect and isn't styled as dangerous.
- **Why it hurts:** Backspace is an easy key to hit by accident, for example when the
  operator thinks a search field still has focus. With a multi-row selection, one keypress
  can wipe out the next hour. Removing the on-air item has undefined on-air behaviour.
- **Fix:**
  - Add undo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z) for playlist edits. That beats a confirm for frequent actions.
  - Until undo exists, confirm when removing more than 1 row, or any locked or on-air row.
  - Never remove the on-air row from the list. Point the operator to Skip instead.
  - Drop Backspace as a delete key, or require Shift+Backspace.

### 4. Transport, carts and stop-all have no keyboard shortcuts — High · M
- **Window:** deck strip, Cartwall (`decks.rs`, `cartwall.rs`). The only key bindings in
  `ui-egui` are Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate and Delete (`context_menu.rs` 13–17).
- **What I see:** there are no hotkeys for Play/Pause per deck, Next, Fade, Skip, carts
  (F1–F12 is the usual broadcast convention) or Stop all carts. The screens show no shortcut
  hints for any of them.
- **Why it hurts:** live operators fire carts and transitions by key, often without looking.
  Mouse-only control is slow and error-prone, and it fails AGENTS.md rule 9 (keyboard
  reachable). Tab focus through dozens of controls is not a realistic on-air path.
- **Fix:**
  - Make each of these a command with a default binding: carts on F1–F12 for the visible
    page, Esc or a dedicated key for Stop all carts, Space for Play/Pause next, and a
    modifier plus key for Fade and Skip so they can't be hit by accident.
  - Show the key on each cart and in the deck button tooltips.
  - Make the bindings editable in the Cartwall ⚙ and Deck ⚙.

### 5. Ads use the same red as ON AIR and danger — Medium · S
- **Window:** main pane, Upcoming, History (`ui-preview.png`, `history.png`).
- **What I see:** Ads dividers and dots are red (#D04848-ish). The on-air row and deck use
  `on_air` #FF5A5F on a dark-red fill, and "stop all carts" uses danger red. Jingles amber is
  close to `warn` (the FADING and PAUSED colour), and News blue is close to `accent` and
  `selection`.
- **Why it hurts:** red should mean only "on air" or "danger". Three red Ads dividers sit
  just above the red on-air row, so a quick glance can latch onto the wrong row. The name
  always shows next to the colour, so rule 9 is met, but the visual priority is muddled.
- **Fix:** keep red, amber and accent-blue out of the playlist palette by default (for
  example, Ads magenta, Jingles gold-green, News cyan-teal), or derive playlist colours away
  from semantic tokens. Add a check that each playlist colour is a minimum ΔE away from
  `on_air`, `warn` and `accent`.

### 6. Past items look the same as future ones; the on-air row is buried — Medium · S
- **Window:** main pane (`ui-preview.png`).
- **What I see:**
  - Eleven already-aired rows (14:34–14:52) are drawn at full brightness above the on-air
    row, which sits roughly at row 11 of 19.
  - Only the on-air row's tint shows where "now" is. There's no "now" line and no visual
    split between played and upcoming.
  - Upcoming starts at 15:00, so the right pane has no "now" row either.
- **Why it hurts:** the eye lands on the top of the list, which is history. Finding "what's
  next" takes a scan.
- **Fix:**
  - Dim played rows (muted text, or hide them behind "show N played").
  - Auto-scroll so the on-air row is the 2nd–3rd visible row.
  - Draw a thin "now" rule with the current time between the on-air and next rows.
  - Mark the next item with a "NEXT" chip.

### 7. Which deck plays next is not shown — Medium · S
- **Window:** deck strip (`ui-preview.png`).
- **What I see:** B (News), C (Juniper Vale) and D (The Quiet Engines) all read `READY ·
  m:ss`, with the same style.
- **Why it hurts:** in Assist or Manual mode the operator must know which deck to fire.
  Even in Auto they need to check that the right item is queued before the hard 15:00.
  Here B holds the 15:00 News and C is the next song, but nothing says that.
- **Fix:** label the decks `NEXT`, `AT 15:00:00 ⏱` and `QUEUED`, with each deck's start
  time (the estimate from the timeline), and highlight the NEXT deck's edge in `accent`.

### 8. The AUTO chip looks like a button but isn't one, and there's no way to change mode — Medium · S–M
- **Window:** deck strip (`decks.rs` 269–290).
- **What I see:** `AUTO` is an accent-bordered pill, styled like the selected tab and
  toggle buttons, but it's a plain Frame plus label. The spec's Assist, Manual and "Stop
  after current" (§6.15) appear nowhere in the UI.
- **Why it hurts:** the operator will click it expecting to switch to Assist and nothing
  happens. In an emergency there's no visible way to stop automation.
- **Fix:** make it a real segmented control (AUTO · ASSIST · MANUAL) or a menu button, with a
  confirm when leaving AUTO. Add a "Stop after current" toggle beside it. Until then, style it
  as a static badge (no border, muted fill) so it doesn't look clickable.

### 9. Mixer: settings replace the live meters; the Out strip is misaligned and its Mute is not distinguished — Medium · M
- **Window:** Mixer (`mixer.png`, `mixer-settings.png`).
- **What I see:**
  - Opening ⚙ shows "Mixer settings" in place of every strip. The meters and faders
    disappear, and the routing list is cut off after "B" with no visible scroll affordance.
  - The Out strip starts about 3 pt lower than the others: its meter, fader and dB lines sit
    lower, and its Mute/PFL row is clipped by the window edge.
  - Mute and PFL are bare text toggles with no frame in the off state, so they read as
    labels.
  - Out's Mute looks exactly like a channel Mute, yet it silences the whole programme.
  - The meters have tick marks but no dB scale labels.
- **Why it hurts:**
  - Changing a device on air blinds the operator to levels.
  - Muting Out by mistake is dead air.
  - Mute or PFL state that can't be seen invites the question "why is B silent?"
- **Fix:**
  - Open settings as a side panel or a separate window that leaves the strips visible.
  - Align the Out strip with the others and fit the window to its content.
  - Draw Mute and PFL as framed toggles with an obvious on state (`danger_fill` for Mute,
    `warn` for PFL) and the text changing too ("MUTED").
  - Give Out Mute a guard (hold or confirm), or remove it.
  - Label the meter scale at 0, −6, −12, −20 and −40 dB.

### 10. Stop all carts sits inside the cart grid's rhythm, and its tooltip doesn't say what it does — Medium · S
- **Window:** Cartwall (`cartwall.png`, `cartwall.rs` 113–124).
- **What I see:** the red "■ stop all carts" is placed about 10 pt under the Bell/Cheer
  carts and is about the same size as a cart. Its only tooltip is "preview — no audio". Its
  label is lower-case, while the carts use Title Case.
- **Why it hurts:**
  - It should be reachable in a panic, but not hit when aiming at the bottom-right cart.
  - The current spot is easy to hit by accident and not especially easy to find in a hurry.
  - A tooltip that only says "preview" reads as if the button does nothing.
- **Fix:**
  - Put Stop all in the header row, or in a full-width bar separated from the grid by
    `space::XL`.
  - Label it "■ Stop all carts" and give it a key (Esc).
  - Use the tooltip text "Stop every playing cart now (Esc)", and add the preview note
    after it.

### 11. Carts don't show length, colour or a playing countdown — Medium · M
- **Window:** Cartwall (`cartwall.png`).
- **What I see:** 16 identical grey buttons with a name only. The source supports cart
  colours and ducking (`cartwall.rs` 190), but none of it shows at rest. The playing state
  isn't shown in any screenshot.
- **Why it hurts:**
  - The operator can't tell a 0:02 Horn from a 0:30 Bed before firing it.
  - In a dense grid, colour plus name is the fastest way to find a cart.
- **Fix:**
  - Show the length (mono, CAPTION, bottom-right) and the cart colour as a left edge bar or
    fill, with the name always visible.
  - While playing, show a progress fill and the remaining time.
  - Show the bound hotkey (finding 4) in a corner.

### 12. Rules editor: "Delete rule" is a plain button and Apply changes the schedule blind — Medium · S
- **Window:** Rules editor (`rules-editor.png`, `rules_editor.rs` 193).
- **What I see:**
  - "Delete rule 1" is a neutral button directly under the constraint row and looks like
    "+ Add rule". "Delete playlist…" uses `danger_item` and a confirm; this doesn't.
  - The sentence builder reads "every N tracks · 1 · between tracks · take 1". The header
    "RULES · trigger · placement · take · constraints" is grammar, not guidance.
  - Apply gives no preview of how it changes the next hour.
- **Why it hurts:** editing rules during a show reshapes what plays next. Deleting a rule,
  or applying an unclear one, can drop the station IDs or ads that are owed.
- **Fix:**
  - Style "Delete rule" with `danger` and move it to the row's right edge as a 🗑 icon with
    a hint.
  - Rephrase as a sentence: "Every [1] track(s), insert [1] item between tracks. Keep the
    same artist [3] apart · Not back-to-back."
  - Above Apply, show "Next 3 placements: 15:05:00, 15:08:12, …" so the result is visible
    before committing.

### 13. Script tab: Apply is the bright primary button while the note says edits don't take effect — Medium · S
- **Window:** Rules editor, Script tab (`rules-script.png`, `rules_editor.rs` 15, 89–102).
- **What I see:** a filled-accent "Apply" sits under muted text that reads "Script edits
  take effect when the script engine lands. Visual view shows the last generated rules."
- **Why it hurts:** this breaks AGENTS.md rule 7 in spirit. A bright Apply reads as "this
  will work". The note is developer jargon ("when the script engine lands") and is easy to
  miss.
- **Fix:** while scripts aren't executed, disable Apply on this tab, or relabel it "Save
  script (not run yet)" in a neutral style. Put the note in a `warn` callout: "Scripts are
  stored but not run in this build. The Visual rules are what plays."

### 14. Two different "+" buttons: tiny, dim, unlabelled, and silent on where items go — Medium · S
- **Window:** main pane header and right pane tab bar (`ui-preview.png`, `add-menu.png`).
- **What I see:**
  - The "+" next to "Main: Music ⚙" opens Add files / Add folder / Add URL / Add live input
    / From Library.
  - The "+" after the Beds tab creates a new playlist.
  - Both are small low-contrast glyphs. The tab "+" in particular looks disabled; measure
    its contrast.
  - The Add menu doesn't say where items will land (end of list? after the selection?).
    The row context menu has "+ Add below" for that.
- **Why it hurts:**
  - The spec's "+ Add item" was a labelled button, and the glyph alone is easy to miss.
  - The same symbol means two different things a few centimetres apart.
  - Adding a 60-minute URL to the end of a 72-track list, when the operator wanted it next,
    is a scheduling error.
- **Fix:**
  - Label them "+ Add" and "+ Playlist", with tooltips.
  - Raise the contrast of the tab "+" to the 3:1 control minimum.
  - Head the Add menu with "Insert after: <selected row> / at end".

### 15. Row context menu: Remove isn't marked dangerous, and some actions are right-click only — Medium · S
- **Window:** main pane context menu (`context-menu.png`).
- **What I see:**
  - 15 items. "Remove  Delete" sits in the Cut/Copy/Duplicate group with normal styling,
    while playlist deletion uses `danger_item`.
  - Play next, Load to deck, Lock position, Properties (metadata) and the deck badge menu
    are reachable only by right-click (the badge has no visible affordance).
  - "Properties" opens a window titled "… — metadata".
- **Why it hurts:**
  - Remove, the dangerous item, is camouflaged among routine actions.
  - The most useful on-air actions (Play next, Load to deck) are hidden behind a right-click
    the operator may not try.
- **Fix:**
  - Put Remove in its own last group with `danger_item` styling.
  - On row hover, show inline "▶ next" and "→ deck" icon buttons.
  - Rename "Properties" to "Edit metadata…".
  - Give the deck badge a hover hint ("Right-click for deck menu"), or add a ⋯ button.

### 16. Clock window: the countdowns are small next to the time of day; inconsistent formats and wording — Medium · S
- **Window:** Clock (`clock.png`).
- **What I see:**
  - The time of day is huge. "News in 02:00" (blue) and "Song ends in 02:00" (white) are
    about a third of its size, and squeezed at the bottom.
  - The formats differ: `02:00` here, while the deck says `−2:00` and lists say `2:00`.
  - It doesn't say whether the News is hard or soft, or its exact time.
  - "Song" is wrong when an ad or jingle is playing.
  - The dotted seconds ring is very faint; measure its contrast.
- **Why it hurts:** the Clock is meant to be the "glance from across the room" display. The
  countdowns are the part that's acted on, so they should dominate as the event nears.
- **Fix:**
  - Give the countdowns their own large lines: "⏱ 15:00:00 News (hard) in 2:00" and "Item
    ends in 2:00".
  - Use one m:ss format across the app.
  - Escalate the size and colour in the last 30 s, with the text changing too.
  - Raise the contrast of the ring's tick dots.

### 17. Add URL shows a red error before anything is typed — Low · S
- **Window:** Add URL dialog (`add-url.png`).
- **What I see:** with "https://" still showing as the placeholder, the red text "the
  address must start with http:// or https://" is already displayed and Add is disabled.
  "Planned length" defaults to 60 min, and "Keep alive" is checked by default.
- **Why it hurts:** an error on a pristine form looks like something is already wrong.
  60 min is an arbitrary default that will silently over-book the timeline if not changed.
- **Fix:**
  - Validate on blur or on the first Add attempt.
  - Prefill "https://" as editable text, not a placeholder.
  - Leave the length empty and required, or default it to the gap before the next fixed
    event.
  - Capitalise the error sentence.

### 18. Terminology drifts across windows — Low · S
- **What I see:**
  - "Main: Music" (main pane), "Music" (History playlist column and Library category) and
    "main" (History origin).
  - "From Library…" (Add menu), "Insert from library…" (context menu) and "Show in Library".
  - "Remove" (rows) versus "Delete" (rule, playlist).
  - "START" (main) versus "AIRED AT" (History).
  - History origin values "main" and "rule" are cryptic.
  - The Library "Folders" tree lists categories (Music, Jingles…), not folders.
- **Fix:**
  - Pick one word per concept and use it everywhere: "From library…" in both menus.
  - Rename origin values to "Main playlist", "Rule", "Manual" and "Fixed".
  - Rename the tree to "Categories", or show real folders.
  - Keep "Remove" for list items and "Delete" for whole objects, and say so in the
    style guide.

### 19. Divider rows: a leading "–" that reads as minus; length inline instead of in LENGTH — Low · S
- **Window:** main pane (`ui-preview.png`).
- **What I see:**
  - Inserted items render as "–14:39:25  Jingles · Station ID 1 (0:06) ———". The en-dash
    before the time looks like the "−2:00" remaining-time sign.
  - Lengths sit in parentheses mid-row while track rows right-align length.
  - The fixed-time glyph ⏱ is glued to the time ("⏱15:00:00").
- **Fix:** drop the leading dash. Put the divider's length in the LENGTH column (muted).
  Add a thin space after ⏱, and put the ⏱ in a fixed-width slot so all times stay
  column-aligned.

### 20. "fit −0.4 s ✓" is engineer language — Low · S
- **Window:** Upcoming (`ui-preview.png`).
- **What I see:** fixed rows end with green mono "fit −0.4 s ✓". Failures use ✖ and red
  (`scheduled_pane.rs` 299–310), so colour isn't the only signal; that part is good.
- **Why it hurts:** a new operator won't know what "fit" means, or whether −0.4 s is early
  or late.
- **Fix:** word it as "on time (−0.4 s)" or "starts 0.4 s early ✓", with the hard/soft
  tolerance in the tooltip. Consider showing it only when it is outside ±1 s.

### 21. Metadata window: Apply vs "Save to file" is unclear; "Hide" doesn't say what it sends — Low · S
- **Window:** Metadata (`metadata.png`).
- **What I see:**
  - Three buttons (Apply, Save to file, Cancel) without saying what Apply does that Save
    doesn't.
  - Stream title "Hide" doesn't say that it sends the station name.
  - The preview `"Juniper Vale - Satellites of Ulvenik"` uses a hyphen, while the app and
    spec use an en dash "–".
  - "Cue points: read-only until the library engine decodes files." is developer jargon.
- **Fix:**
  - Rename the buttons to "Apply to this item" and "Write tags to file".
  - Rename "Hide" to "Station name", or add the hint "(sends the station name)".
  - Make the preview use the real separator.
  - Reword the note as "Cue points can't be edited in this build".

### 22. Library: "Dock into main window" looks like a label; truncated paths — Low · S
- **Window:** Library (`library.png`).
- **What I see:**
  - "Dock into main window" is plain text, while "+ Add folder…" next to it has a frame.
  - The PATH column truncates mid-name ("music/Mira Sole…"); I didn't confirm whether there
    is a tooltip.
  - Every title has a ☰ drag glyph, which adds noise.
  - The "drag a title onto a pane, a playlist or a cart" hint is good. The item context menu
    (`library.rs` 393) gives a keyboard-free alternative, but nothing on screen says so.
- **Fix:**
  - Give "Dock" a button frame, or make it an icon with a hint.
  - Show the full path on hover.
  - Show ☰ only on row hover.
  - Extend the hint to "…or right-click → Add to".

### 23. Status bar: warnings are easy to miss — Low · S
- **Window:** main window status bar (`ui-preview.png`).
- **What I see:** "Local ○ preview (no audio)   Icecast ○ not connected   · 0 warnings" in
  small muted text at the bottom edge. The hollow circles correctly avoid a fake green
  (rule 7). "· 0 warnings" starts with a stray separator and isn't clickable as far as the
  render shows.
- **Why it hurts:** when warnings exist (stream dropped, missed event), the operator's eyes
  are on the deck strip, not the bottom-left corner.
- **Fix:**
  - When there are warnings, also show a `warn` chip next to AUTO in the deck strip.
  - Make the count open the warning list.
  - Remove the leading "·".

### 24. Tool windows repeat their title, and their header rows aren't consistent — Low · S
- **Window:** Cartwall, Mixer, Clock, History and Library.
- **What I see:**
  - Each shows the title twice: in the OS bar and as a heading ("Cartwall / Cartwall").
  - The header rows differ:
    - Cartwall puts its preview note, pages and ⚙ on one line.
    - Mixer puts the preview note on a second line.
    - History has no label on its filter field.
    - Library has a "Search" label.
- **Fix:** drop the in-window heading when the window has an OS title bar. Use one header
  template: filters/tabs left, status note muted, ⚙ far right, and labels on all search
  fields.

## Per-window notes (not already covered)

- **Main window:**
  - The deck title truncation can't be checked; all sample titles fit. Make sure long
    "Artist – Title" truncates with an ellipsis and has a tooltip.
  - "72 tracks · next 6 hours" is useful.
  - The Upcoming cards (a bordered box per row) are visually heavier than the Main list.
    Consider plain rows with a hairline to match the left pane.
- **Cartwall:** the Page 1 / Page 2 tabs are clear. With a large wall, show the page count
  ("1/3") as in the spec.
- **Mixer:**
  - Strip names use mono, unlike other headings. That's fine, but be consistent.
  - The stacked "−14.7 dB / pk −11.8 dB / gain +0.0" lines are dense; consider showing only
    peak on the strip and the rest on hover.
  - "Live input channels  L 1  R 2" reads like two buttons; label them as fields.
- **Clock:** good, simple layout; the right window for a second screen.
- **History:**
  - Clean and well aligned; mono times line up.
  - It has no date, so a log crossing midnight is ambiguous. Add a date group header or a
    date column.
  - The last row is cut mid-row at the window edge; a fade or row-snapped height would help.
- **Rules editor:** the Visual / Script / Items & source tabs are clear. The pointer cursor
  in the render hides part of "every N tracks"; that's not an issue.
- **Context menu:** the shortcut labels show "Ctrl+X". Check that they render as ⌘X on macOS.
- **Add menu:** "Add live input…" and "Add URL…" should say where they insert (finding 14).

## What I couldn't judge

- **States not in any screenshot:**
  - FADING, PAUSED and EMPTY decks.
  - A failed fit (✖).
  - A missed fixed event.
  - A dropped stream or reconnecting row.
  - A playing cart.
  - Non-zero warnings.
  - The Confirm dialog.
  - Selection linking between the panes.
- **Screens not shown:**
  - Per-playlist tabs (Jingles, Ads…) and the playlist right-click menu.
  - The "Items & source" tab.
  - Every ⚙ panel except Mixer (Deck, Cartwall, Clock, History, Library, Out/Icecast).
  - The Library drop target and the Library context menu.
- **Not visible in stills:**
  - Keyboard focus order and focus rings.
  - Hover tooltips, except where I read them in source.
  - Motion and flashing.
- **Contrast:** I didn't measure it. The tokens (`text_muted` #9CA5B4 on #0E1014) and
  `Tokens::readable()` should keep body and playlist text at or above 4.5:1. The dim "+"
  tab, the disabled "Add" button and the Clock's tick ring look marginal; measure them.
- **Scale:** the renders are 2x. I judged sizes against the theme's point sizes, not
  physical size on a real monitor at operator distance.
